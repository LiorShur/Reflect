## What Reflect is

Reflect is a mobile app that facilitates structured conversations between
two paired users. It uses an AI assistant to suggest softer wording for
messages before your partner sees them, and to summarize what each partner
said at the end of a session.

- **Not a safety tool for abusive relationships.** If you feel unsafe with

## AI-generated content

Reflect uses AI (Anthropic's Claude) to generate softened versions of
your messages and to summarize sessions. AI output may be inaccurate,
awkward, or miss nuance. You are the final decision-maker on what your
partner sees — every AI-suggested message shows you a "send original"
option with equal weight.
