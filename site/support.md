## Safety resources

If you are in immediate danger, please contact local emergency services.

If you need to talk to someone about relationship abuse or safety:

- **United States**: [988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org)
  (call or text 988); [National Domestic Violence Hotline](https://www.thehotline.org)
  (1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788)
- **United Kingdom**: [Samaritans](https://www.samaritans.org) (call 116 123);
  [Refuge](https://www.refuge.org.uk) (0808 2000 247)
- **Canada**: [Talk Suicide Canada](https://talksuicide.ca) (1-833-456-4566);
  [Ending Violence Association of Canada](https://endingviolencecanada.org)
- **Australia**: [Lifeline](https://www.lifeline.org.au) (13 11 14);
  [1800RESPECT](https://www.1800respect.org.au) (1800 737 732)
- **Israel**: [ERAN](https://www.eran.org.il) (1201);
  [WIZO Domestic Violence](https://wizo.org.il) (1-800-353-300)

Reflect is a communication tool, not a substitute for professional care
or crisis response.

## Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account. This removes your account, screening, pairing,
session history, appreciation feed, and baseline. Your partner's data
stays with them.
