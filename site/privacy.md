# Privacy policy

## Who we are

Reflect is a mobile app that helps two partners have structured conversations.
This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, who we
share it with, and the choices you have.

Contact for privacy questions: **REPLACE WITH EMAIL** (e.g., privacy@reflect.app)

## What we collect

**Account data**
- Email address (used to sign in and to recover your account)
- A unique account identifier issued by our authentication provider

**Pairing data**
- The account identifier of the partner you have paired with
- The timestamp at which you paired

**Conversation data during a session**
- Text you type in the compose field (the "raw" message)
- The softened version generated by our AI assistant
- The listener's reflection ("mirror") of what they heard
- Your check-in scores (a self-rated 1–10 activation score)
- Any per-session flags produced by our moderator

**Session history**
- After a turn completes, the delivered message and the listener's
  reflection are archived so we can produce a wrap-up summary
- Wrap-up summaries produced by our AI at session end

**Appreciation feed**
- Messages you send your partner as daily appreciations, plus optional tags

**Screening**
- A tier (low / moderate / high) computed from a one-time safety check-in.
  The raw answers to the check-in are **never stored** — only the tier and
  the list of question IDs that contributed to it.

**Telemetry**
- One-way hashes (SHA-256) of AI inputs and outputs so we can debug our
  prompts without retaining raw content. Includes latency and cost per call.
- The plain text of AI conversations is **not** stored in telemetry by
  default.

**Baseline**
- A rolling average of your message length and punctuation use, so the
  moderator can detect when your messages diverge from your normal style.

## What we do with it

- Route messages between you and your partner during a session
- Run the moderator + translator + summarizer AIs to help conversations
  land better
- Show your appreciation feed
- Compute your safety tier from screening answers
- Improve our AI prompts over time using the hashed telemetry

We **do not**:
- Sell your data
- Share your conversations with advertisers
- Use your conversations to train third-party AI models

## Who we share it with

**Firebase (Google Cloud)**
- Stores your account, session data, and telemetry
- Handles authentication
- Runs our server code
- Google's own privacy policy applies to the underlying infrastructure

**Anthropic**
- Receives the text of messages you compose (the "raw" version) so it can
  generate the softened version, and receives the archived history at
  session end for the wrap-up summary
- Per Anthropic's data policy, API inputs are not used to train Anthropic's
  models
- See Anthropic's [privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)

We do not share your data with any other third parties for their own use.

## How long we keep it

- **Account data**: until you delete your account
- **Session drafts and current turn**: cleared at the end of each session
- **Session history**: retained for as long as you have an account, so you
  can review past sessions
- **Telemetry (hashed)**: 24 hours by default. Longer retention is opt-in.
- **Screening tier**: until you delete your account
- **Appreciation feed**: 90 days rolling history on your device; older
  entries are pruned server-side

When you delete your account:
- Your account, screening, pairing, session history, appreciation feed,
  and baseline are permanently removed
- Your partner's data stays with them (they may have references to your
  removed content in their history)
- The delete flow requires re-entering your password to confirm

## Your rights

You can, at any time:
- **Access** your data by reading it in the app
- **Correct** your profile fields directly in Settings
- **Delete** your entire account from Settings → Delete account

If your jurisdiction gives you additional rights (e.g., GDPR portability,
CCPA "do not sell" — noting we do not sell your data regardless), contact
us at **REPLACE WITH EMAIL** and we will respond within a reasonable
timeframe.

## Age

Reflect is intended for adults (18+). Do not create an account if you are
under 18. If we learn we have collected data from someone under 18, we
will delete it.

## Safety and mandatory reporting

Reflect is a communication tool, not a substitute for professional
counseling. If you are in danger, please contact local emergency services
or a domestic-violence hotline. Resources are listed at
[**REPLACE WITH RESOURCES URL**].

We may be required to disclose information in response to lawful
requests from authorities.

## Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this
document and notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
